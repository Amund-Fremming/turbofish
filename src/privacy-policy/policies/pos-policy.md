# Pøs - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 5, 2026_

**Contact:** for support, questions, or data requests, email [amund.fremming@gmail.com](mailto:amund.fremming@gmail.com)

## Data controller

Pøs is developed and operated by Amund Fremming as an individual, not a registered company. Contact: amund.fremming@gmail.com.

## What data we collect

- Home and work address, both as text and as coordinates (latitude/longitude)
- Departure times, commute duration in minutes, and which weekdays you commute
- A randomly generated user ID, created when you complete setup in the app
- A push notification token from Apple/Google, if you enable notifications

The app does not use device location services (GPS) and never requests location permission — the addresses are what you type in yourself.

## Why we collect it

Solely to calculate the weather forecast shown in the app, and to send a notification before you leave if rain is forecast. Nothing is used for advertising, and the app contains no analytics or tracking.

## How long we keep it

For as long as you use the app. Deleting the app from your phone removes the local link on your device, but the row in our database is not deleted automatically. If you want your data deleted, email [amund.fremming@gmail.com](mailto:amund.fremming@gmail.com) with the address you used, and it will be deleted manually.

## Sharing with others

Addresses are never sent to any third party. Coordinates are sent from our own server to a weather service to fetch a forecast — the weather service receives only coordinates, never your name, email, or user ID.

## Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email address above.
